## Key idea
All living things are made of cells. A cell is the smallest unit of life that can do the work of life.

Parts found in animal and plant cells:
- **Cell membrane**: controls what goes in and out.
- **Cytoplasm**: where many chemical reactions happen.
- **Nucleus**: controls the cell's activities.

Plant cells also have a **cell wall**, **chloroplasts** (where photosynthesis happens) and a large **vacuole**.

## Worked example
A microscope has an eyepiece lens of $\times 10$ and an objective lens of $\times 40$.
Total magnification = eyepiece $\times$ objective = $10 \times 40 = 400$.
The cell looks 400 times bigger than it really is.

## Practice
- Draw a plant cell and an animal cell side by side. Label each part.
- List two differences between them.
- Find the magnification for an eyepiece $\times 10$ and objective $\times 10$.

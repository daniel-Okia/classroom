## Key idea
Integers are whole numbers that can be positive, negative or zero, like $-3, 0, 5$.
On a number line, **adding** moves you to the right and **subtracting** moves you to the left.
Subtracting a negative number is the same as adding a positive number: $a - (-b) = a + b$.

## Worked example
1. $-3 + 5$: start at $-3$ and move 5 steps right. You reach $2$.
2. $4 - 7$: start at $4$ and move 7 steps left. You reach $-3$.
3. $2 - (-3)$: subtracting a negative becomes adding. $2 + 3 = 5$.

## Practice
Work these out on a number line in your book:
- $7 + (-10)$
- $-2 - 5$
- $-8 - (-3)$
- $6 - (-6)$

## Check yourself
- Can I say which way to move on the number line for $+$ and for $-$?
- Can I explain why $2 - (-3)$ is $5$?
- Can I tell which is greater, $-2$ or $-7$?

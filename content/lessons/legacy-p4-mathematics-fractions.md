## Key idea
A fraction names equal parts of a whole. In $\frac{3}{4}$ the bottom number (denominator) says the whole is split into 4 equal parts. The top number (numerator) says we take 3 of them.

▮▮▮▯ is $\frac{3}{4}$ shaded.

## Worked example
Find $\frac{3}{4}$ of 12.
1. Divide 12 into 4 equal parts: $12 \div 4 = 3$.
2. Take 3 parts: $3 \times 3 = 9$.
So $\frac{3}{4}$ of 12 is 9.

## Practice
- Find $\frac{1}{2}$ of 10.
- Find $\frac{1}{4}$ of 20.
- Find $\frac{2}{3}$ of 9.

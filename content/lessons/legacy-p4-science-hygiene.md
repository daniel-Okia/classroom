## Key idea
Personal hygiene means keeping our body clean so that germs do not make us sick. Germs are tiny living things that can cause disease.

Good habits:
- Wash hands with soap and clean water **before eating** and **after using the toilet**.
- Bathe every day.
- Keep nails short and clean.
- Brush teeth morning and night.

## Worked example
Amina plays outside, then comes to eat.
1. Her hands may carry germs from the soil.
2. She washes them with soap and clean water for about 20 seconds.
3. Now it is safer to eat.

- Say why long nails can make us sick.
